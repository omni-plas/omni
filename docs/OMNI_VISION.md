# Omni Vision

Omni is a universal digital creation and compilation platform.

## Core capabilities

1. **Universal Digital Creation** — model, validate, construct, and materialize any digital entity, including entities that do not yet exist or have not yet been invented.
2. **Universal Compilation** — represent source programs independently of surface syntax and compile them toward existing or future targets through extensible semantic IR, frontend, and backend capabilities.

Languages, platforms, execution models, and digital-entity kinds are extensible capabilities rather than a closed hard-coded list.

## Engineering principle

These goals must become verifiable behavior. UIR validation, semantic analysis, IR lowering, optimization, target resolution, artifact generation, and tests are first-class parts of Omni rather than demo-only interfaces.
